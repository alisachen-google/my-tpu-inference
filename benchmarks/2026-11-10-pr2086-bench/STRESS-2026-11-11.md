# PR 2086 stress test: mixed load to failure, drain, verify (2026-11-11)

Head `83198bc912`, the same tree, image and recipe as the campaigns in `README.md`. The brief: 2P2D, TP16 on both
roles, early send on, prefix caching and KV offload on the prefill, input length random in 1..256k, 1..64 images,
text and images, short and long and GSM8K, raise the load until the system starts to fail, drain, verify everything
passes, quality gate, no hang, no crash, 3 or 6 hours.

**What ran to completion: a 3-hour run on 1P1D with two-host TP16 engines** (`j-cee50ceb-f5ed-44f4-80f9`,
18:15-21:23Z). The 2P2D TP16 layout (four two-host slices at once) was never admitted by the cluster in 6 hours of
queueing, and the 2P2D fallback on single-host TP8 engines was spot-preempted seven times and only ever reached its
seventh ramp level; its partial data is kept. Details of every attempt are in the last section.

## Setup

- Model `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct` (the only 256k-context multimodal model in the bucket; gpt-oss-120b takes no
  images), served name `qwen3-vl-8b`, context 262,144, page size 128, KV fp8, images capped at about 400 tokens each
  (`--mm-process-config {"image":{"min_pixels":65536,"max_pixels":313600}}`).
- Engines: one prefill and one decode, each on two tpu7x 2x2x1 hosts of one 2x2x2 slice (`TOPOLOGY=2x2x2`, TP16,
  `--nnodes 2`), `--max-running-requests 64`, decode batch buckets precompiled up to 64. HiCache host tier over Raiden
  on both roles, `write_back`, prefill host pool ratio 1.2, decode 2.0 (the first attempt used 3.5, see F3). Switch on
  (`SGLANG_ENABLE_PREFILL_KV_OFFLOAD_TO_DECODE_DRAM=1`), early send on
  (`SGLANG_DISAGG_PREFILL_EARLY_SEND_CACHED_PREFIX=1`), decode radix cache on.
- Driver (`harness/stress_driver.py`, stage `harness/stage_stress.sh`): a seeded request mix through the random
  router and the pinned (prefill, decode) router. Per request: text with `input_ids` of a random length (40% in
  1..256, 25% in 257..8k, 20% in 8k..128k, 15% in 128k..261k) through `/generate`; or 1..64 synthetic JPEGs of random
  size plus random text through `/v1/chat/completions` (35% of new requests); or a GSM8K question (8%, greedy, answer
  checked); or a return of an earlier session, its prompt plus its output plus a new block, through the router that
  served it (30%); 5% of streaming requests are closed by the client after the first token; outputs 1..1,024 tokens
  (10% up to 2,048) with `ignore_eos`; 70% greedy. Concurrency ramps 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 14, 21, 32, 48, 72, ... (x1.5),
  300 s per level. A level ends the ramp when its error rate is above 5%, a request hangs (no completion for 600 s),
  an engine answers no `/health` probe three times in a row (30 s timeout each), or TTFT p50 passes 600 s. Then:
  drain (in-flight requests finish, up to 1,200 s), every engine idle (no running, queued or in-transfer requests for
  30 s), a verification batch of 24 mixed requests at concurrency 8 that must all succeed with their full output, the
  GSM8K gate (100 fixed questions, must stay within 5 pp of the idle baseline), a `flush_cache` on every engine and
  the leak check (`token_usage` below 2% afterwards). The next cycle starts from concurrency 2 with a new seed until
  the 3 hours are up. Every request, level and cycle is recorded; the verdict is in
  `results/h83198bc/stress_1p1d_tp16_vl_3h/*_stress_verdict.json`.
- Host memory of every engine pod was sampled every 5 minutes with `kubectl top` from the VM
  (`evidence/h83198bc/stress_1p1d_tp16_vl_3h/mem_kubectl_top.log`, until the VM's credentials expired at 19:40Z, so the
  samples cover cycle 1 and the start of cycle 2).

## Result of the 3-hour run (1P1D, two-host TP16 engines)

0 and 60 s, so that condition is saturation, not a crash (F4). The nine requests that did not complete were aborted by the engines on the KV transfer deadline (F2).

## Findings

- **F1. Saturation, not failure, is what the ramp finds first.** A single two-host prefill is prefill-bound on this
  mix from about 6 concurrent requests on (TTFT p50 32 s at 6, 121 s at 32, 218 s at 48, 336 s at 72); throughput does
  not collapse and nothing errors until 48. The engines never hung: the stall detector (no completion for 600 s with
  requests in flight) never fired in 3 hours, and every drain emptied the queues within 76 s.
- **F2. At concurrency 48, 9 requests were aborted by the engines when the KV transfer hit its 60 s deadline.**
  (Corrected 2026-11-16: first reported as "HTTP 200 with zero output tokens, silent". The driver had not read the
  stream's finish reason; per the code both engines abort such a request with status 500 and a streaming client gets
  that as the final chunk. See ISSUES I9.) All nine are at concurrency 48 (2 in cycle 1, 1 in cycle 2, 6 in cycle 3),
  prompts 14k to 261k tokens. Engine logs: `Prefill transfer failed ... DEADLINE_EXCEEDED phase=await_pull` and
  `Decode transfer failed`: the saturated decode did not pull within the deadline. This is the system "starting to
  fail" under overload, reported to the client; it is not an offload defect.
- **F3. Decode host RAM: the 3.5 host-pool ratio leaves no headroom for load; the growth is per host and released on
  drain.** The first 3-hour attempt (`j-d470c991-e1c0-4012-aa47`, decode HiCache ratio 3.5) passed concurrency 2..32
  and was killed at 48 when decode host 1 hit its 900 GiB cgroup limit (`OOMKilled`). At ratio 3.5 the decode's host
  pool is 406 GB per host (2,752,640 fp8 tokens per rank, 8 ranks) on top of the Raiden pool, the eight ranks'
  resident set and the request payloads. At ratio 2.0 the completed run shows the same shape with room to spare:
  decode host 1 goes 477 GB at concurrency 2, 606 at 32, 729 at 48, 791 at 72 (peak sample), and returns to 530 GB
  after the drain, while decode host 0 stays within 468..510 GB throughout; the prefill shows the same asymmetry
  (host 1 627 GB at 48 and 748 at 72, host 0 under 500). So the growth is proportional to the in-flight load, is
  released, and lands on the second host of each engine (ranks 8..15), which is where the per-host budget has to be
  set. ISSUES I10.
- **F4. `/health` starves under saturation and under multimodal preprocessing.** At 72 concurrent requests the
  prefill answered no health probe within 30 s for over 90 s (cycles 1 and 2); during the 20-minute smoke a single
  60-image, 237k-token request held `/health` for more than 15 s while the tokenizer process preprocessed its images.
  Liveness probes with the usual 10..30 s budgets would restart a healthy, saturated engine. ISSUES I11.
- **F5. The offload's proactive pushes time out when the decode is saturated.** `Raiden host transfer victim:...
  failed (DEADLINE_EXCEEDED, await_pull): Transfer session timed out` 17 times on the prefill's rank 0 and 4 on the
  decode's, clustered at the top of each ramp: the prefill offered a victim branch, the decode did not pull it within
  60 s, the session was dropped. No request is affected (the branch is simply not offloaded), the counters stay equal
  across ranks, but each is a wasted host lock and session on a busy decode. Same family as I8. ISSUES I12.
- **F6. Head-of-line blocking of short requests.** With chunked prefill of 8,192 tokens and no priority scheduling,
  a 100-token GSM8K question waits behind the 256k prompts ahead of it: GSM8K TTFT p50 49.5 s over the run against
  0.2 s on idle engines. Expected for this scheduler; worth knowing when sizing the prefill count for a mixed workload.
- **F7. Quality held.** GSM8K baseline 96 of 100 on idle engines; 95, 96 and 97 after the three ramps, with 2 or 3
  answers changing per check (greedy decoding under batching, the same effect the campaigns' background probes show),
  and 141 of 151 inline questions correct under load. No leaked KV: `token_usage` 0 on both engines after every flush;
  0 bootstrap failures.

Environment problems met on the way, none of them the PR's: Kueue cannot assign a workload that carries a required pod
(anti-)affinity on topology-aware flavors (the recipe's affinity, copied from `sglang-raiden-pd-stress`, removed; the
webhook's `podset-required-topology` already keeps an engine on one slice); the PR tree needs `zstandard`, absent from
the 20261103 image (installed at start); three of five two-host bring-ups needed the recipe's TPU-init retry
(`START_SESSION failed`, the previous tenant's session); seven spot preemptions on the single-host pools in 3 hours;
Kueue preempts a user's second active workload for any cdk-p0 arrival ("first active workload guarantee"); the VM's user
credentials expired mid-run (observation only, the run and its files were unaffected).

## Recommendations for the next round

1. Run the same stage on 2P2D TP16 when four 2x2x2 slices can be held (off-peak, as the user's only workload), and a
   6-hour run at a fixed concurrency just under the saturation knee (about 32 for this 1P1D) to look for drift.
2. For F2, nothing to fix in the engines (the abort is reported in-band); the driver now records the finish reason
   so an aborted stream is counted as "server abort 500" instead of short output.
3. Set the decode's HiCache ratio from the per-host budget: pool plus Raiden pool plus 8 ranks' resident set plus
   about 250 GB of in-flight staging at 48..72 concurrent 256k requests on the second host.
4. Keep the prefill's `/health` cheap, or give probes a budget above 90 s when the engine is saturated.

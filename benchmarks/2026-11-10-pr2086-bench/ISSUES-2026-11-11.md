## I3. Spot preemptions at startup (cluster)

- Symptom: of the four 1P1D jobs, three lost a node within minutes of placement, before or just after the engines
  started: `j-ae4dd88f-6893-4c7d-8796` (write-back off) twice (`UnschedulableOnAssignedNode` at 23:59:03 and 00:04:50),
  `j-ca202e3d-91f2-44d7-991e` (write-through on) twice (`TerminationByKubelet` at 23:58:36 and, three minutes into its
  second run, at 00:39:59), `j-8c1912b8-b43d-463f-8d17` (write-through off) once (`TerminationByKubelet` at 23:59:51).
  First log line: `Non-Kueue node preemption detected (TerminationByKubelet). Tearing down and re-queuing job (1/30)`
  in `cdk job desc`.
- Cause: the single-host tpu7x pools had 2 free nodes of 51 at submit time and 8 nodes NotReady; new placements land
  on spot nodes that are reclaimed. The recipe cannot pin non-spot nodes (CDK rejects the node affinity).
- Workaround: job_manager's automatic retry (up to 30); the harness starts every stage from clean result files, so a
  retried attempt does not mix with the interrupted one (`fresh_run` in `common.sh`). No data point was taken from an
  interrupted attempt.
- PR defect: no.

## I4. `write_back: backup failed under host memory pressure but subtree drop declined (node locked)` on the on arm

- Symptom: on the write-back **on** arm (`j-74143469-8c3a-4bd6-9575`) the prefill logged 96 such lines (12 per TP
  rank), in three bursts: 6 at 00:08:54 during the 12-session recall return (the point whose hit was 37.1%), 1 at
  00:12 during the 16-session return, 5 at 00:29:43 during the integrity run's recall phase. The off arm
  (`j-ae4dd88f-6893-4c7d-8796`) logged none. The line is one of the five invalidating patterns of the brief
  ("subtree drop declined").
- First log line (TP0): `[2026-11-11 00:08:54 TP0 EP0] write_back: backup failed under host memory pressure but
  subtree drop declined (node locked); root node 646 stays device-resident until host space frees`, preceded by
  `Prefill batch, #new-seq: 2, #new-token: 8185, #cached-token: 139264, #host-hit-token: 139264, full token usage:
  0.99`.
- Reading: with the switch on, a push in flight host-locks the whole path of the pushed leaf until the decode has
  stored it; when write-back then needs host room for a new backup and the LRU victim is on a locked path, the
  eviction is refused and the device copy is kept. No request failed, no out-of-memory followed (the H4 shape of
  2026-11-07 did not recur), and the counters show every push stored (204/204) and landed (204/204). The device pool
  was at 0.99 when the first burst happened, so the refused drops cost device room during that return.
- Whether it is a PR defect: a warning path that the PR's own recall commit was designed to make survivable
  (`adopt_received_backups`); it did not fail here. It is the one engine-side condition that separates the two arms
  during the integrity run (I5), so it is reported with that.
- Status: observed, not fixed, write-back specific: neither write-through arm (`j-ca202e3d-91f2-44d7-991e`,
  `j-8c1912b8-b43d-463f-8d17`) logged one. The fresh-engine integrity run (I5) tells whether it is tied to the mismatch.

## I5. Single-request output equivalence on the write-back on arm: 12 of 16 exact when run after the ladders

- Symptom: `stage_bench.sh` runs the integrity check last, on the same engines that served the recall ladder, the
  static ladder and the mixed-size block (the reference `goal_hybrid_final5.sh` runs it first, on fresh engines).
  On the on arm: 16 sessions filled, recall phase one request at a time with no probes, each output compared with a
  recompute after a flush of both engines: **12 of 16 identical**. Sessions 6, 11 and 14 were served from the
  prefill's host tier (139,264 cached tokens each) and diverge at tokens 5, 5 and 19; session 15 was served from
  nowhere (0 cached tokens, a full recompute) and still diverges from its recompute at token 22. On the off arm, same
  procedure after the same ladders: **16 of 16 identical**, all 16 served from the prefill's host tier.
- The prefill batches of both paths have the same chunk boundaries (recall: 139,264 cached + one 8,192 chunk + 59
  tokens; recompute: 17 chunks of 8,192 + 8,192 + 59), on both arms, so chunking does not explain it.
- Background probes (6 concurrent 8k requests during fill and settle) disagree with their own reference in 16 of 91
  (on) and 16 of 96 (off) cases at the same positions: TPU greedy decoding is not batch-invariant, as seen before;
  that is why the recall phase runs one request at a time.
- Session 15 (no recall, still different) says at least part of the divergence is engine state rather than the
  transferred pages. The candidates that differ between the arms at that moment: the refused subtree drops of I4
  (device-resident locked nodes) and the hybrid SWA host tier, which is on only with the switch.
- Both write-through arms, same procedure after the same ladders: 16 of 16 identical each.
- Follow-up running: the same integrity check on **fresh** engines of the on arm, write-back
  (`j-c909ba56-55e8-4b10-8eb3`, `STAGE=edges EDGES=E1`), the way the reference and the PR's own 16/16 were obtained.
- PR defect: open until the fresh-engine run is in. If fresh engines give 16/16 and the post-ladder state does not,
  the report names it as a state-dependent divergence of the on arm (I4's condition), to reproduce on bare metal.

## I6. Harness: static points had no throughput in-job (harness, fixed)

- Symptom: `point_metrics.py` printed `total None tok/s` for the static ladder inside the first four jobs.
- Cause: `static_reuse.py` records carried no request start time, so the point's wall could not be derived from the
  jsonl.
- Fix: the client now records `t_start`; `point_metrics.py` falls back to the client's own `wall_s` from its
  summary. The first four jobs carried the old copy (map-dir snapshot at submit), so their points were recomputed on
  the VM from the raw jsonl, snapshots and summaries (`recompute_points.py`, output `points_fixed.jsonl` under
  `results/`); the raw files are unchanged.
- PR defect: no.
